Keep the reviewer token and all account settings out of the recording.

Show Overview after signing in. Explain that GigBid matches jobs to portfolio
evidence, screens risk and drafts proposals, while the freelancer approves every
send. Identify the demonstration data as synthetic.

## 0:20-1:15 - One job to approved proposal

node progress, requirement/evidence mapping, risk report and pricing rationale.

dry-run cost, approve once, and show submitted state and Connects balance.

occurs. Do not claim the regex catches every possible attack.

approval pause. Approve the response and show sent status. Explain that this is
a local message, not an actual client conversation.

## 2:15-2:40 - Durability and negotiation

approval. If there is time, enter a rate negotiation below the configured floor
and show the proposed counteroffer before approval.
